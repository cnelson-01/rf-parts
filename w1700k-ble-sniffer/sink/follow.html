Follow devices by address

Type or paste one or more Bluetooth LE addresses above (any format: AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF, aa-bb-…, aabbccddeeff) or search by name. Up to 8 devices.

The page shows every packet between them and where a connection attempt breaks down, telling apart:

  1. the central never sent a connection request (it only scans, or it talks to someone else),
  2. it sent one and the sniffer decoded it, but nothing followed (the peripheral did not answer, or the central gave up),
  3. it sent one and the sniffer could not decode it (a collision in the reply slot, a weak signal, a PHY the sniffer does not decode, a capture gap),
  4. the two were already connected before the window,
  5. the address is not the one on air (resolvable private addresses change about every 15 minutes).

The sniffer decodes LE 1M only and does not decrypt.
